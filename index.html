The platform wasn't properly responsive across screen sizes, game thumbnails were inconsistent in size and broke the visual rhythm of the lobby, and slow JPG/PNG images dragged down load times. Worse, the site would run fine for 10–15 minutes and then progressively lag due to an unidentified memory leak — degrading the experience the longer someone played.

The Goal

Fix the responsiveness, visual consistency and performance problems at the root, then introduce features that would give players new reasons to engage — without touching what already worked well.

UX Case Study

Innodigital eKYC — Onboarding UX

Designed the end-to-end user experience for a digital KYC onboarding flow serving ICICI & Axis Bank customers — simplifying a legally complex process into a clear, 3-step journey.

RoleUI/UX Designer & Dev
ToolsFigma · Design Tokens
DurationMay – Oct 2024
The Problem

Digital KYC is a legally sensitive, document-heavy process. The legacy onboarding flow buried mandatory verification steps inside a confusing 7-step form, leading to high abandonment before customers ever reached the bank's actual product.

The Goal

Design a compliant, trustworthy onboarding experience that felt as simple as any modern consumer app — without cutting a single regulatory step required by RBI or SEBI.

Process
manage_search
01

Research & Constraints

  • Analysed RBI and SEBI eKYC compliance guidelines to map mandatory data
  • Identified key failure points in legacy bank onboarding
account_tree
02

Flow Design

  • Compressed 7-step legacy flow into 3 clearly labelled stages
  • Designed 20+ Figma wireframes across mobile and desktop breakpoints
palette
03

Visual Design System

  • Built a token-based component library — inputs, OTP screens, document upload states — reused across builds
  • Defined an accessible colour and type scale meeting WCAG 2.1 AA contrast requirements
fact_check
04

Usability Testing & Handoff

  • Tested prototypes with first-time digital-banking users to surface confusing copy and error states
  • Delivered developer-ready specs, redlines and a design-token handoff document
Outcomes
7 → 3 Onboarding stages, fully compliant
AA WCAG 2.1 accessibility compliance
20+ Wireframes across breakpoints
2 Bank platforms reusing the design system
